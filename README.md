# CFEES Incinerator Design & Process Optimization

## Overview

This project focuses on the design and process optimization of a
dual-chamber liquid waste incinerator for the safe treatment of
hazardous liquid waste, carried out at the Centre for Fire, Explosive
and Environment Safety (CFEES), DRDO.

## Key Work

- Studied the complete incineration process from waste feeding to
  combustion and flue gas treatment.
- Designed a dual-chamber incinerator with primary and secondary
  combustion stages.
- Performed mass and energy balance calculations for fuel consumption,
  heat losses and useful heat output.
- Analyzed emission-control systems including Venturi scrubber,
  ID fan and Continuous Emission Monitoring System (CEMS).
- Proposed optimization strategies including combustion-air preheating,
  improved insulation and flue-gas heat recovery.

## Key Results

- Fuel energy input: 80,000 kcal/hr
- Useful thermal output: 66,848 kcal/hr
- Calculated gross efficiency: 83.6%
- Optimized efficiency: 89.9%
- Air-to-fuel ratio analyzed: 15:1
- Primary chamber temperature: ~850°C
- Secondary chamber temperature: 1100–1300°C
- Residence time: 2 seconds
